# Tone presets

Seven presets. Each changes scripting energy, pacing, typography personality, and transition style.
A freeform creative direction from the user always refines or overrides the preset.

| Tone | Energy | Scenes | Per scene | Transitions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `default` | Playful, clean, postable | 4–5 | 3–5 s | crossfade / clean slide |
| `polished` | Serious, elegant, restrained | 3–4 | 4–6 s | slow crossfade 0.6–0.8 s |
| `yc-parody` | Deadpan startup launch, played straight | 4–5 | 3–4 s | hard cut / 0.2 s crossfade |
| `chaotic` | Fast, loud, unhinged | 6–8 | ≤4 s, some <2 s | hard cut, flash, zoom |
| `deadpan` | Calm, dry, nothing registers as unusual | 3–4 | 4–7 s | very slow crossfade 0.8–1.0 s |
| `cinematic` | Dramatic, trailer-scale | 4–5 | 3–5 s | dramatic wipe, scale-in 0.95→1.0 |
| `app-store` | Clean, professional, feature-forward | 4–6 | 3–4 s | clean slide/wipe 0.35–0.45 s |

---

## `default`

**Voice:** first person plural, warm, direct, no corporate language.
**Typography:** mixed case, comfortable weight, words breathe.
**Hook:** a simple question or observation that sets up the reveal.
**Highlights:** short punchy phrases, one idea per scene.
**Outro:** product name, then a tagline. Light punchline.
**Use for:** most products with personality that don't try too hard.

## `polished`

**Voice:** third person or none — the product speaks for itself.
**Typography:** mixed case, light-to-medium weight, generous letter-spacing, nothing aggressive.
**Hook:** a single strong image, or the product name at full scale.
**Highlights:** one feature per scene. No bullets, no lists.
**Outro:** product name. Tagline. Silence.
**Use for:** things that aren't jokes; anything that should feel premium.

## `yc-parody`

**Voice:** serious, matter-of-fact, no winking. The absurdity is the material's, not the tone's.
**Typography:** sentence case, medium/heavy, monospace-adjacent for data points, no decoration.
**Hook:** the problem, stated completely seriously.
**Highlights:** a feature or metric stated as fact.
**Outro:** product name, tagline, a URL that implies legitimacy.
**Use for:** recursive or absurd concepts that benefit from being played straight.

## `chaotic`

**Voice:** aggressive. SHORT WORDS. CAPS. Metric dumps. Confidence is louder than exclamation marks.
**Typography:** ALL CAPS, heavy, slightly oversized; some words tilted or larger than expected.
**Hook:** something that shouldn't exist, at full volume.
**Highlights:** rapid-fire — one word or one number per beat.
**Outro:** the name slams in, tagline hits, cut to black.
**Use for:** hype-reel material, speed, urgency, logistics parodies.

## `deadpan`

**Voice:** minimal — one observation, then the product. That's it.
**Typography:** mixed case, large, sparse, lots of empty space, one thought at a time.
**Hook:** a quiet observation. No setup, no punchline yet.
**Highlights:** one sentence per scene. No bullets. No excitement.
**Outro:** the product name. Nothing else. Maybe a very small tagline. Long hold on empty space.
**Use for:** anything where restraint makes the point land harder.

## `cinematic`

**Voice:** epic, short declarative sentences, each landing before the next begins.
**Typography:** ALL CAPS or heavy mixed case, full-bleed, large type, significant scale.
**Hook:** a sweeping statement about the world, stated seriously.
**Highlights:** capabilities stated like superpowers.
**Outro:** product name slams in full-screen, tagline, implied music swell.
**Use for:** material with natural epic quality — scale, grand claims, big stakes.

## `app-store`

**Voice:** feature-benefit, third person, present tense.
**Typography:** title case, medium weight, clean and readable, no aggression.
**Hook:** product name + tagline, clean.
**Highlights:** feature-card structure — name, then 1–2 supporting details.
**Outro:** CTA-style. "Available now." + a qualifier.
**Use for:** B2B-parody or productized concepts that benefit from being taken seriously as a product.
