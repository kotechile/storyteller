# Brag creative laws

These apply to every promo video regardless of tone. They are the non-negotiables of `/brag`,
ported here so the Remotion pipeline enforces the same standards the Hyperframes skill did.

**Short.** 15–25 seconds. Not one second more without a reason. This holds whether or not narration
is on; narration does not extend the window.

**Readable.** Keep the pace high through motion and cuts, never by flashing text. Every line a
viewer must read holds long enough to read it:

- a short label or 1–3 word line: ~0.8 s **settled** (entered, not yet exiting);
- a headline or full sentence: ~0.3 s per word, minimum ~1.2 s;
- the hook line gets the most time of all.

Fast-in, then hold. Never fast-in, then gone. Entrances and transitions stay snappy (0.3–0.6 s) while
the settled hold is generous — that reads as punchy *and* legible.

Two failure modes to design out:

- **Too much text for the scene length.** A 4 s scene lands maybe 2–3 short reads, not 6. If a scene
  carries more text than its duration allows, **cut copy or split the scene — never speed it up.**
- **Sequential text snapped onto a fast beat.** Beat-grid reveals can land ~0.5 s apart at 110+ BPM.
  That is fine for accents (glows, ticks, dots) but too fast for readable lines. For sequential
  *text*, hold each item to the reading floor.

**Specific.** The video must feel like it was made for this exact URL, not any URL. Use the actual
copy, claims, and vocabulary found in the source.

**Show the thing.** At least one scene must display actual UI, real copy, or a key visual from the
product — no abstract filler. The product *doing* its thing beats the product *describing* its thing.

**No generic SaaS language.** "Streamline your workflow", "unlock insights", "supercharge your team"
are banned. Use the project's real words.

**The hook is everything.** The first 2 seconds decide whether someone keeps watching. Plan the hook
before anything else.

**Funny earns its place.** Humor comes from the material's own absurdity, never from straining to be
funny. If the source material is earnest, play it straight.

**Explain, don't just sell.** This pipeline's mandate is *promote **and** explain*. Every video names
2–4 concepts the source actually teaches and gives them real screen time. A promo that sells a thing
without teaching anything about it has failed this pipeline's brief.

**Nothing secret goes on screen.** The source may contain secrets, tokens, internal hostnames, real
customer names, emails, or personal data. Those must NEVER appear in the plan, the on-screen text,
the generated narration, or the share copy. Substitute plausible fictional stand-ins and say so in
the plan's `notes`. This is a hard rule: these videos get published publicly.

**Pattern (adapt, don't obey):**

```
Hook (2-3s) → Reveal (2-4s) → 2-4 sharp highlights incl. concept beats (5-15s) → Punchline/outro (2-4s)
```

The pattern is a starting shape, not a template. Not every source needs exactly three highlights.
